# Award sources and year semantics

## Source priority

Use official award sources for result truth:

- Hugo: `thehugoawards.org`, especially the year-specific Hugo history page.
- Nebula: `nebulas.sfwa.org` year and category archives, with `sfwa.org` winner announcements as a second official confirmation.

Use publisher or author-controlled pages only for publication metadata, story context, and legal reading routes. Do not let an external article override an official award result.

When two official pages disagree, preserve the catalogue and report `NEEDS_REVIEW` with both URLs.

## Archive years

- Hugo archive years are ceremony years.
- Nebula archive years label the eligible work year. A Nebula presented in 2026 for work published in 2025 belongs in the catalogue as `year: 2025` and should link to the official `award-year/2025` archive.

Do not convert one award's convention into the other's or derive the archive year from the page publication date.

## Reading routes

Verify that a reading route exposes the complete story, not an excerpt, preview, index, purchase page, or search result. Prefer:

1. Original publication or publisher-authorized reprint (`publication`).
2. Legitimate library or web archive (`archive`).
3. Another complete external copy whose provenance has been reviewed (`third-party`).

Use `reading: null` when no acceptable route is available. Never fabricate or promote an unclear mirror merely to avoid a missing link.
