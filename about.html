About the collection

What is included, how records are checked, and how to report a problem.

Scope

A focused reading index

This collection is built to cover Hugo and Nebula Award winners for Best Short Story. The current release contains Hugo results from 2001 through 2025, including the 2015 No Award result.

Nebula results for the same period are planned next. The collection excludes finalists, other award categories, summaries, recommendations, and ratings.

Methodology

How records are checked

Award years, titles, authors, and publication details are checked against the official archive for each award. Reading links are included only when a complete text can be identified on a publisher, magazine, or author-controlled website.

A missing reading link does not mean the story is unavailable in print or paid digital editions. It means this collection did not verify a public full-text page.

Each story appears once. If it wins more than one award, the same record shows every award with that award's own year and official source; those years may differ.

Independence and copyright

An external-link catalogue

SciFi Short Story Collection is an independent project and is not affiliated with the Hugo Awards, the Nebula Awards, the World Science Fiction Society, the Science Fiction and Fantasy Writers Association, any Worldcon, author, or publisher.

The site does not reproduce story text, cover art, or award logos. Copyright in each story remains with its author or rights holder. Reading and award links lead to external websites.

Corrections

Help keep the archive accurate

A corrections email will be added before publication. Until then, this deployment-ready build is not accepting public submissions.
