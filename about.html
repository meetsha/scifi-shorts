About this collection

The Hugo and Nebula Awards have recognised science fiction and fantasy for generations. This collection brings their short-story winners together in one place, creating a reading path through seven decades of the genre.

The catalogue follows the Hugo Awards’ short-fiction lineage from 1955 and the Nebula Awards’ Best Short Story category from 1965, through 2025. The Hugo category was called Short Fiction from 1960 to 1966 and was not presented in 1957. A story that won both awards appears once, with both wins shown. Learn more about the Hugo Awards and Nebula Awards.

Award details are checked against official archives. Reading links open selected external sources and may change over time. This independent site does not host story text and is not affiliated with either award.
