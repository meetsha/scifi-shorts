About the collection

What is included, how records are checked, and how to report a problem.

Scope

A focused reading index

This release covers Hugo Award results for Best Short Story from 2001 through 2025. It lists the winning story for each year, except 2015, when Hugo voters selected No Award.

The collection currently excludes finalists, other Hugo categories, Nebula winners, summaries, recommendations, and ratings.

Methodology

How records are checked

Award years, titles, authors, and publication details are checked against the official Hugo Awards archive. Reading links are included only when a complete text can be identified on a publisher, magazine, or author-controlled website.

A missing reading link does not mean the story is unavailable in print or paid digital editions. It means this collection did not verify a public full-text page.

Independence and copyright

An external-link catalogue

SciFi Short Story Collection is an independent project and is not affiliated with the Hugo Awards, the World Science Fiction Society, any Worldcon, author, or publisher.

The site does not reproduce story text, cover art, or award logos. Copyright in each story remains with its author or rights holder. Reading and award links lead to external websites.

Corrections

Help keep the archive accurate

A corrections email will be added before publication. Until then, this deployment-ready build is not accepting public submissions.
